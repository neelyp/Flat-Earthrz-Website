Core Gameplay Loop

Harvest, toss, build, feed, rescue.

Harvesting

Blue gathers wood, fiber, and crystal. Red gathers stone, ore, and coal. Each side has resources the shared hub needs.

Logistics

Toss materials across the void into the hub stockpile. If the hub does not have the recipe, the next structure cannot be built.

Biofuel

Blue aliens need fiber. Red aliens need coal. Keep both Biofuel meters alive or an entire population can starve out.

Rescue Windows

Five meteor showers shrink the outer worlds. During the rescue window, carry aliens to the hub before the impact lands.

Flat Worlds

Three islands, one civilization to save.

Flat Earthrz starts after two species crash-land on opposite flat worlds. The center hub is their only long-term home, so players must build defense and sustainability structures there before the fifth meteor shower destroys the outer islands.

Verdant Glade

Blue-only island stocked with trees, vine bushes, and crystal nodes. Fiber from this side feeds the blue population.

Molten Ridge

Red-only island with rocks, ore veins, and coal deposits. Coal from this side keeps the red population fed.

Central Hub

Shared safe zone with the crafter, furnace, stockpile, and rescue space. Structures appear here as the build queue advances.

Mechanics & Controls

Two control schemes, one shared emergency.

Action What it does
Harvesting P1 uses WASD and E. P2 uses arrow keys and Enter. Stand near a node to harvest resources.
Tossing P1 presses Q and P2 presses Period to throw the selected item toward the central hub stockpile.
Processing P1 presses T and P2 presses M at the machine to process materials such as planks, bricks, ingots, and rope.
Build / Rescue P1 presses F and P2 presses Comma at the crafter to build. Tab and Right Shift pick up aliens during rescue windows.

Design Goals

Readable, urgent, and built around shared survival.

  • Top-down isometric 2D presentation keeps the three flat worlds and leap routes legible.
  • The HUD tracks current build, hub stockpile progress, defense, sustainability, Biofuel, and shower timer.
  • Difficulty changes the time between showers: Easy gives 8 minutes, Normal gives 5, and Hard gives 3.

Win Condition

Build everything and save every alien.

To win, players must finish all 12 hub structures and have all five blue aliens and all five red aliens safe on the hub when the final meteor shower destroys the outer worlds.
